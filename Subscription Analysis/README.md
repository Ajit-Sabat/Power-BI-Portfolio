# 🎬 OTT Subscription Analysis

## 📋 Project Overview

This project analyzes subscription patterns, user behavior, and revenue metrics across three major OTT (Over-The-Top) streaming platforms: **Netflix**, **Amazon Prime Video**, and **JioHotstar**. The interactive Power BI dashboard provides comprehensive insights into subscriber demographics, churn patterns, revenue distribution, and regional performance across multiple years (2019-2025).

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- **Subscription Metrics:** Track total subscribers, active subscribers, and churn rates
- **Revenue Analysis:** Analyze revenue patterns by subscription plans and demographics
- **User Behavior:** Understand average watch time, app ratings, and usage patterns
- **Risk Management:** Identify high-risk subscribers by tenure and behavior
- **Geographic Analysis:** Compare subscriber distribution across countries
- **Platform Comparison:** Benchmark performance across Netflix, Prime Video, and JioHotstar

## 💡 Key Findings & Insights

### 1. Subscriber Patterns

**Churn Rate Analysis:**
- JioHotstar has the highest churn rate at **23.70%**
- Prime Video has the lowest churn rate at **20.84%**
- Netflix sits in the middle at **22.14%**
- All platforms show consistent high-risk user percentage around **9.3-9.7%**

**Subscriber Distribution:**
- JioHotstar leads with **3,084 total subscribers** (largest user base)
- Netflix: **2,159 subscribers**
- Prime Video: **1,406 subscribers** (smallest but most premium)

### 2. Revenue & Monetization

**ARPU Comparison:**
- Netflix commands highest ARPU: **₹6,783** (premium positioning)
- JioHotstar: **₹3,308** (mid-market)
- Prime Video: **₹2,230** (value positioning)

**Total Revenue:**
- Netflix: **₹11.40M** (highest revenue despite smaller base)
- JioHotstar: **₹7.78M**
- Prime Video: **₹2.48M**

**Plan Preferences:**
- Netflix: Premium plans dominate (53%), indicating willingness to pay more
- Prime Video: Annual plans heavily favored (83%), showing commitment
- JioHotstar: Balanced across Mobile (34%), Super (25%), and Premium (41%)

### 3. Demographic Insights

**Revenue by Gender & Age Group:**

**Netflix:**
- Young Adults (25-34): Highest revenue at **₹1.3M** per gender
- Gen-Z (18-24): **₹1.2M** revenue contribution
- Adults (35-44): **₹1.2M** revenue
- Middle-Aged (45-54): **₹0.6M** revenue
- Gender split relatively balanced across age groups

**Prime Video:**
- Young Adults (25-34): **₹0.32M** revenue per gender
- Adults (35-44): **₹0.28M** revenue
- Gen-Z (18-24): Significant contributor
- Balanced male/female distribution

**JioHotstar:**
- Young Adults (25-34): Largest segment at **₹1.1M** per gender
- Adults (35-44): **₹0.6M** revenue
- Strong presence across all age groups (18-64)
- More balanced demographic spread

### 4. Subscriber Risk Analysis by Tenure

**Risk Bifurcation Pattern (All Platforms):**
- **0-3 months:** Highest risk period with maximum churn
- **4-6 months:** Risk decreases significantly
- **7-12 months:** Moderate risk, users becoming stable
- **13-24 months:** Lower risk, established subscribers
- **40-60 months:** Very low risk, loyal long-term users
- **25-36 months:** Stable base
- **61-72 months:** Minimal churn
- **37+ months:** Ultra-stable subscribers

**Insight:** First 3 months are critical for retention - highest high-risk users in this period

### 5. Geographic Distribution

**Active Subscribers by Country (Combined Overview):**

**Top Markets:**
1. **India:** Largest market across all platforms
   - JioHotstar: 1,766 subscribers
   - Prime Video: 279 subscribers
   - Netflix: 424 subscribers
   
2. **USA:** Second largest market
   - Netflix: 335 subscribers
   - Prime Video: 254 subscribers
   - JioHotstar: 92 subscribers

3. **Other Countries:** Combined smaller markets
   - Netflix: 303 subscribers
   - Prime Video: 180 subscribers
   - JioHotstar: 188 subscribers

4. **UK:** Significant presence
   - Netflix: 187 subscribers
   - Prime Video: 85 subscribers
   - JioHotstar: 70 subscribers

5. **Canada, Brazil, Australia, Germany:** Smaller but consistent markets

**Platform-Specific Geographic Strengths:**
- **JioHotstar:** Dominates Indian market (1,766 subscribers)
- **Netflix:** More globally distributed (USA: 335, India: 424)
- **Prime Video:** Strong in India (279) and USA (254)

### 6. Temporal Trends (2019-2025)

**Subscribers and Churn Rate Over Time:**
- All platforms show **seasonal patterns** in subscriber counts
- **Churn rate fluctuates** between 20-30% across months
- **Total subscribers** (blue bars) generally higher than **active subscribers** (teal bars)
- Peak months show increased acquisition but also higher churn

**Monthly Average Watch Time:**
- Ranges between **43-52 hours** across platforms
- Prime Video August peak: **50.5 hours**
- JioHotstar shows consistent **45-50 hours** range
- Netflix maintains steady **49-54 hours** throughout year

### 7. Refund Analysis

**Platform Comparison:**
- Netflix: ₹0.23M refunds (₹23,160K from Feb spike)
- Prime Video: ₹0.04M refunds (₹14K peak in Jan)
- JioHotstar: ₹0.14M refunds (₹24K peak in Feb)

**Refund Pattern:**
- Early year (Jan-Mar) shows highest refund amounts
- Refunds decrease significantly mid-year
- Netflix has highest absolute refunds but best net revenue retention

## 💼 Business Recommendations

### 1. Reduce Early Churn (0-3 Month Subscribers)
**Problem:** Highest risk and churn in first 3 months  
**Recommendation:** 
- Implement onboarding email series
- Offer personalized content recommendations
- Create "first month engagement" campaigns
- Monitor usage in first week and trigger interventions

### 2. Optimize Pricing Strategy
**Insight:** Netflix's high ARPU (₹6,783) vs Prime's (₹2,230)  
**Recommendation:**
- Netflix: Maintain premium positioning
- Prime: Consider premium tier introduction
- JioHotstar: Optimize mobile plan pricing for volume

### 3. Platform-Specific Geographic Expansion
**Data-Driven Strategy:**
- **JioHotstar:** Double down on India (1,766 subs), expand to Tier 2/3 cities
- **Netflix:** Strengthen USA/India presence, target UK/Canada growth
- **Prime Video:** Leverage India base (279), grow USA market

### 4. Annual Plan Promotion
**Insight:** Prime Video's 83% annual plan adoption  
**Recommendation:**
- Netflix/Hotstar: Incentivize annual plans with discounts
- Reduce monthly churn by converting to annual
- Target 40-50% annual plan mix

### 5. Content Strategy by Demographics
**Young Adults (25-34) drive highest revenue across all platforms**
- Focus content acquisition on this demographic
- Create targeted marketing campaigns
- Analyze content preferences for retention
